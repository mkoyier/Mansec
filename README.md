## 🔐 What I do

I hunt for vulnerabilities in smart contracts and DeFi protocols through competitive audits, and I write findings up as clear, submission-ready reports: root cause, impact, proof of concept, and a fix that actually works.

Before Web3 security, I spent ~9 years in operations and supply chain. That background taught me to think in systems, spot where processes break, and communicate risk to people who have to act on it. I bring the same mindset to protocol audits.

## 🎯 Focus areas

- **Reentrancy & CEI violations**
- **Access control flaws** (missing or broken permission checks)
- **Oracle & price manipulation**
- **Flash loan attack surfaces**
- **Weak randomness & on-chain predictability**
- **Signature issues** (malleability, replay)
- **Unchecked ERC20 returns, storage collisions, integer pitfalls**

## 🧪 Codebases I've worked through

`PuppyRaffle` · `ThunderLoan` · `MyCut` · `DatingDapp` · `Beatland` · `Vanguard` · `Snowman Merkle Airdrop`

Every one of these was a chance to practice the full loop: scope the code, build a threat model, write a PoC, and report it.
